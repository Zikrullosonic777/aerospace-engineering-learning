# Aerodynamics Research ✈️

## Objective
Study how air interacts with objects, particularly aircraft wings and airfoils.

## Topics
- Lift
- Drag
- Air resistance
- Air pressure
- Airflow
- Airfoil geometry
- Bernoulli's principle

## Research Questions
1. What generates lift?
2. What factors influence drag?
3. How does airfoil shape affect airflow?
4. How does airspeed affect aerodynamic forces?

## Planned Activities
- Research aerodynamic principles.
- Study different airfoil shapes.
- Collect and organize scientific data.
- Create explanatory diagrams.

## Learning Outcomes
Develop a fundamental understanding of aerodynamics.
